Description

An exceptional and beautifully extended four-bedroom semi-detached family home, occupying a generous plot with outstanding kerb appeal, substantial gated driveway and attractive front and rear gardens, all set back from the road in a desirable and established residential location.

Finished to an impressive standard throughout, this fantastic home has been thoughtfully enhanced by the current owners to create a stylish, spacious, and highly versatile living environment, perfectly suited to modern family life. From the moment you arrive, the property's attractive frontage, quality presentation, and welcoming appearance, immediately sets it apart from all other properties, in its own a standalone class.

The accommodation begins with an entrance porch leading into a welcoming hallway reception. To the front of the property is an elegant and comfortable reception lounge, providing the perfect space to relax and unwind, whilst a separate study offers an excellent home office, playroom, or additional reception area to suit a variety of lifestyles.

Practicality has not been overlooked, with a fitted utility room, ground floor WC and an invaluable storage room providing excellent day-to-day convenience. The true centrepiece of the home is the stunning high-specification kitchen, thoughtfully designed with both style and functionality in mind. This impressive space opens seamlessly into a substantial family room extension, flooded with natural light from overhead Velux windows and offering direct access to the rear garden. Ideal for entertaining guests, family gatherings, or simply enjoying everyday living, this sociable open-plan space perfectly reflects the demands of contemporary family living.

Externally, the property continues to impress. The beautifully landscaped rear garden provides a wonderful extension of the living space, featuring attractive patio areas for outdoor dining and entertaining, alongside well-maintained lawns offering plenty of room for children to play, and families to enjoy. Side gated access enhances both convenience and practicality.

To the first floor, the spacious master bedroom benefits from a stylish en-suite shower room, while a generous second double bedroom, and well-proportioned third bedroom provide excellent accommodation for growing families. The fourth bedroom is currently utilised as a luxurious dressing room with fitted storage space but can easily be reinstated as a bedroom should a purchaser require. A contemporary family bathroom completes the first-floor accommodation on offer.

Situated within the highly sought-after suburb of Abbeydale, the property enjoys the benefits of a well-established and family-friendly community. Abbeydale remains one of Gloucester's most popular residential areas, offering excellent local schooling, nearby amenities, parks, and recreational facilities, together with convenient access to Gloucester city centre, the M5 motorway and surrounding countryside. The location is particularly attractive to families and professionals alike, combining a peaceful residential setting with exceptional convenience for commuting and everyday living. Families and outdoor enthusiasts will also appreciate the wealth of green spaces close at hand, including the renowned Robinswood Hill Country Park, a 100-hectare countryside haven offering scenic walks, nature trails, play areas and breathtaking views across Gloucester, the Severn Vale, and the Cotswolds.

Offering an outstanding blend of space, quality, style, and location, this remarkable family home presents a rare opportunity to acquire a property that is truly ready to move into. Homes of this calibre in Abbeydale are rarely available for long, and early viewings are strongly recommended to fully appreciate everything this exceptional property has to offer.
