Description

Offered to the market with no onward chain, this two-bedroom mid-terrace property presents an excellent opportunity for buyers seeking a home with significant scope for improvement and value enhancement.

Requiring some modernisation, the property offers well-proportioned accommodation comprising a lounge to the front and a spacious open-plan kitchen/dining room, with bathroom to the rear, providing an excellent foundation for renovation as desired to suit modern tastes and lifestyles. To the first floor are two good-sized bedrooms with potential to improve and add value through cosmetic upgrading.

Externally, the property benefits from an enclosed courtyard garden, with shed and rear gated access, providing a private, low-maintenance outdoor space with the potential to create an attractive seating and entertaining area.

Conveniently positioned within easy reach of Gloucester city centre, local amenities, schools and transport links, this property will appeal to first-time buyers looking to create their ideal home, investors seeking a promising addition to their portfolio, or developers looking for a straightforward project.

Properties offering such clear potential in a central and convenient location are increasingly difficult to find, and early viewing is strongly recommended.
